### YouGlish

- Link: https://youglish.com/
- Category: Speaking / Pronunciation

- Limitations: Examples vary in quality and accent; requires active repetition to be effective.
- Relevance to Specularis: Useful for improving pronunciation accuracy, phrase memory, and speaking naturalness.
- Notes: Best used to check how words and phrases sound in real speech before practicing aloud.
