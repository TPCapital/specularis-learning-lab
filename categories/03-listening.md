## Resources
### TED Talks

- Link: https://www.ted.com/talks
- Category: Listening / Comprehensible Input
- Use Case: Improve listening comprehension through speeches, transcripts, subtitles, and topic-based input.
- Strengths: Wide range of topics, clear speech, transcripts available, useful for shadowing and dictation.
- Limitations: Some talks may be too difficult for lower-intermediate learners without repeated listening.
- Relevance to Specularis: Useful for building listening stamina, vocabulary exposure, and topic-based English input.
- Notes: Best used with a repeat-listen-shadow-summarize routine.
